Website analytics & privacy

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits to this website and clicks on download links. A click is not proof that a download or installation finished. GitHub publishes a separate count for release asset downloads.

This site does not set analytics cookies or enable advertising tracking. Vercel processes request information to provide aggregate counts; its visitor hash is discarded after 24 hours. “No cookies” does not mean “no data sent.” Visitor counts are estimates, and blocked requests may be missing.

Event URLs contain no query parameters or fragments. If the incoming referrer contains more than its origin, we skip analytics for that page. We do not collect messages or activity inside the Dispatch app. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and a previously saved refusal prevent our analytics script from loading. Preview and local development sites do not send production events. Google Analytics is not loaded on this site.

网站统计与隐私

本站使用 Vercel Web Analytics 统计浏览和下载链接点击。点击不代表下载或安装完成；GitHub 附件下载数是另一项指标。

本站不设置统计 Cookie，不启用广告追踪。Vercel 会处理请求信息来生成汇总统计，访客哈希在 24 小时后丢弃。无 Cookie 不等于没有数据传送；访客数是估算值，被拦截的请求可能漏计。

事件网址不含查询参数和片段。若来源网址包含域名以外的路径或参数，本次不采集。不读取 Dispatch App 中的消息或活动。浏览器开启 DNT / GPC，或已有拒绝记录时，不加载统计脚本。预览站和本地开发不发送生产事件；本站不加载 Google Analytics。
