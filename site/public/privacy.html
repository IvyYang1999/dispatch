Website analytics & privacy

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits to this website and clicks on download links. A click is not proof that a download or installation finished. GitHub publishes a separate count for release asset downloads.

Analytics storage and advertising consent remain denied. We do not read or write analytics cookies, enable Google Signals or advertising personalization, or create a persistent visitor ID. Google still receives basic events and request information, including the browser's IP address and user agent; “no cookies” does not mean “no data sent.” Counts may be incomplete and cannot reliably identify returning visitors.

We strip URL queries and fragments, send only the origin of the referring website, and do not collect messages or activity inside the Dispatch app. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and a previously saved refusal prevent our analytics tag from loading. Preview and local development sites do not send production events.

网站统计与隐私

本站使用 Google Analytics 4 统计浏览和下载链接点击。点击不代表下载或安装完成；GitHub 附件下载数是另一项指标。

统计存储与广告同意状态始终为拒绝，不读写统计 Cookie，不启用 Google Signals、广告个性化或持久访客 ID。Google 仍会收到基本事件及请求信息（包括浏览器的 IP 地址和 User Agent）；无 Cookie 不等于没有数据传送。统计可能漏计，也不能准确识别回访者。

统计事件去掉网址查询参数和片段，来源只保留网站域名，不采集 Dispatch App 中的消息或使用活动。浏览器开启 DNT / GPC，或已有拒绝记录时，不加载统计标签。预览站和本地开发不发送生产事件。
